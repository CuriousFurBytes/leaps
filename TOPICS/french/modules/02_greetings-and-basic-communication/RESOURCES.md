# Resources: Module 02 — Greetings and Basic Communication

- **[TV5Monde — Apprendre le français A1](https://apprendre.tv5monde.com/fr/apprendre-le-francais/niveaux/a1)** — Free video exercises at A1 level covering introductions, greetings, and daily communication
- **[Forvo — French phrases](https://forvo.com/languages/fr/)** — Hear native speaker pronunciation of any phrase or word including numbers and nationalities
- **[Conjuguemos — Number practice](https://conjuguemos.com/)** — Drill French number recognition and recall, including the difficult 70–99 range
- **[RFI — Journal en français facile](https://savoirs.rfi.fr/fr/apprendre-enseigner/langue-francaise/le-journal-en-francais-facile)** — Free daily B1-level French news; listening to authentic French speech builds number comprehension over time
