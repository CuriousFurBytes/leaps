# Resources: Module 01 — Orientation and Pronunciation

## Core Resources

- [Lawless French](https://www.lawlessfrench.com/) — grammar and usage explanations.
- [Forvo French Pronunciation Dictionary](https://forvo.com/languages/fr/) — native-speaker pronunciation examples.
- [TV5MONDE Apprendre le français](https://apprendre.tv5monde.com/) — listening practice and graded activities.

## Practice Materials

- Keep a personal vocabulary deck with full example sentences.
- Record short voice notes and compare them across weeks.
- Save useful phrases from real conversations in `NOTES.md`.

## Verification Notes

All listed resources are widely known French-learning resources; verify individual pages before citing a specific lesson.
