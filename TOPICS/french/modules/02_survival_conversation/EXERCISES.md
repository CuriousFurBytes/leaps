# Exercises: Module 02 — Survival Conversation

## Instructions

Complete each exercise in order. Exercises increase in difficulty. Speak aloud where prompted and keep brief notes about what felt hard.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Recall key phrases from the module.

Write five French phrases from this module and a one-line context for each.

### Exercise 2

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Practice controlled pronunciation or sentence production.

Record yourself saying the module's example phrases twice: once slowly and once at a natural pace.

### Exercise 3

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Connect form to meaning.

Translate three module examples into natural English, then explain one difference from a word-for-word translation.

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Adapt a pattern to personal information.

Create a six-line mini-dialogue using your real or fictional details.

```text
A: Bonjour...
B: ...
```

### Exercise 5

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Use repair strategies.

Write three phrases you can use when you do not understand, need repetition, or need time to think.

### Exercise 6

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Notice common errors.

Find one likely English-to-French transfer mistake in your own work and rewrite it correctly.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7

**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Perform a realistic interaction.

Run a two-minute practice interaction with a partner, tutor, or voice recorder. Use at least one repair phrase.

### Exercise 8

**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Reflect and revise.

Listen to or reread your interaction and write three corrections for the next attempt.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9

**Difficulty:** Expert
**Objective:** Build independent learning judgment.

Design a one-week micro-plan that reuses this module's material in listening, speaking, reading, and writing.
