## Overview

This capstone asks you to use French with real people or a real audience over time. The artifact is not a worksheet; it is sustained communication with preparation, interaction, repair, and reflection.

You may choose a conversation series, interview project, reading-and-response exchange, voice-message exchange, club participation plan, or written correspondence project. The key requirement is continuity: multiple connected interactions that show you can understand, respond, adapt, and learn.

## Prerequisites

- Modules 01–11 of this topic, especially pronunciation, grammar, listening, writing, culture, and conversation repair.
- A safe partner, tutor, exchange group, class, or community where participation is appropriate.

## Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Design a realistic French communication project with ethical boundaries and clear goals.
- Sustain real interaction across multiple sessions or messages.
- Repair misunderstandings using French strategies rather than switching immediately to English.
- Reflect on evidence of growth and identify the next independent learning cycle.

## Project Brief

Create a sustained French communication project lasting at least two weeks. Complete at least four interactions, each with preparation notes, evidence of participation, and a reflection.

Acceptable formats include:

- Four 20-minute conversation sessions with a tutor or exchange partner.
- A sequence of eight voice messages with a Francophone partner.
- A written interview and follow-up exchange with a French speaker.
- Participation in a French discussion group with a final written reflection.

```text
Goal: Hold four conversations about daily life, work, culture, and future plans.
Repair phrase: Je cherche mes mots. Je veux dire que...
Reflection prompt: What did I understand, where did I break down, and what will I practice next?
```

## Milestones

1. Define the audience, format, schedule, and safety boundaries.
2. Prepare topic cards, vocabulary, and repair phrases.
3. Complete the first interaction and write a reflection within 24 hours.
4. Complete remaining interactions, increasing spontaneity each time.
5. Produce a final portfolio with evidence, transcript excerpts, self-assessment, and next steps.

## Help and Getting Unstuck

Use these hints only when blocked. They are scaffolding, not a finished solution.

### Hint 1: If You Cannot Start

Begin with a scripted opening, then ask one genuine question.

```text
Bonjour, merci de parler avec moi. Aujourd'hui, je voudrais pratiquer les présentations et les questions simples.
```

### Hint 2: If You Freeze

Use a repair phrase, pause, and rephrase with simpler grammar.

```text
Je ne connais pas le mot exact. C'est comme... une petite maison pour les vacances.
```

### Hint 3: If You Need Depth

Ask follow-up questions: Pourquoi ? Depuis quand ? Qu'est-ce que vous en pensez ?

## Assessment

You pass the capstone by demonstrating sustained communication, not flawless French. Evidence should show preparation, interaction, repair, cultural respect, and honest reflection.
