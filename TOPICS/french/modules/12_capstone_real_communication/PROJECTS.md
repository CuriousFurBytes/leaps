# Projects: Module 12 — Capstone Project: Sustained Real Communication

## Capstone Project: Sustained Real Communication Portfolio

Build a portfolio around at least four connected French interactions over two or more weeks.

**Deliverables:**

- Project plan.
- Preparation materials.
- Evidence log.
- Reflection after each interaction.
- Final self-assessment and next-step plan.

**Success Criteria:**

- [ ] Communication is real, sustained, and ethical.
- [ ] Interactions connect to each other rather than standing alone.
- [ ] French repair strategies are used when needed.
- [ ] Reflection identifies specific next practice targets.
