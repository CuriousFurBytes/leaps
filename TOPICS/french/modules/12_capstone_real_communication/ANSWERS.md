# Answers: Module 12 — Capstone Project: Sustained Real Communication

## Answer Key

### Easy Questions

**Q1:** It includes multiple connected interactions over time with preparation and reflection.
**Q2:** Examples include conversation sessions, voice-message exchanges, written interviews, or discussion group participation.
**Q3:** Ethical evidence such as notes, reflections, excerpts, timestamps, and consented recordings.
**Q4:** Real communication involves real people, privacy, and boundaries.
**Q5:** Any appropriate repair phrase, such as `Je cherche mes mots.`

### Medium Questions

**Q6:** A complete script would remove spontaneity and prevent evidence of real communicative judgment.
**Q7:** A good answer defines goals, phrases, topics, boundaries, and likely repair needs.
**Q8:** Reflection turns experience into targeted practice.
**Q9:** Accuracy matters, but passing requires making meaning, repairing breakdowns, and adapting.
**Q10:** Cultural respect affects topics, register, consent, politeness, and expectations.

### Hard Questions

**Q11:** The plan should be specific, safe, scheduled, and measurable.
**Q12:** The corrected plan needs concrete audience, dates, goals, evidence, and reflection.
**Q13:** French should be relevant, polite, and usable.
**Q14:** The response should include a repair phrase and a simpler rephrasing strategy.

### Expert Questions

**Q15:** The answer should synthesize multiple prior modules into one interaction model.
**Q16:** The cycle should use evidence to set focused goals, practice, feedback, and reassessment.

### Bonus Questions
