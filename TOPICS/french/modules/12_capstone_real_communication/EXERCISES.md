# Exercises: Module 12 — Capstone Project: Sustained Real Communication

## Instructions

Treat these as capstone milestones. Do not copy a finished script; build your own communication plan and adapt it after real feedback.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Define a safe communication context.

Choose your audience, format, schedule, and boundaries.

### Exercise 2

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Prepare reusable support phrases.

Write ten French phrases for opening, asking follow-up questions, repairing misunderstandings, and closing.

### Exercise 3

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Set evidence standards.

Decide what evidence you can ethically keep: notes, timestamps, reflections, short excerpts, or recordings with consent.

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Complete the first real interaction.

Hold or send the first interaction and write a reflection within 24 hours.

### Exercise 5

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Revise based on feedback.

Update your topic cards and vocabulary after the first interaction.

### Exercise 6

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Sustain continuity.

Complete a second interaction that refers back to something from the first.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7

**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Increase spontaneity.

Complete two more interactions with less scripting and more follow-up questions.

### Exercise 8

**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Analyze breakdowns.

Identify three moments where communication failed or slowed, then write better repair strategies.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9

**Difficulty:** Expert
**Objective:** Produce a capstone portfolio.

Assemble evidence, reflections, self-assessment, and a next-step learning plan.
