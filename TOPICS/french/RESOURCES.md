## Official References

- **[Académie Française — Dictionnaire](https://www.dictionnaire-academie.fr/)** — The official dictionary of the French language, published by the Académie Française. Use for authoritative spelling and usage. (9th edition, ongoing)
- **[TV5Monde — Learn French](https://apprendre.tv5monde.com/en)** — Free French learning resources from the international French-language television network. Includes exercises at all CEFR levels, video content with transcripts, and cultural content. Verified free.
- **[RFI Savoirs — Langue française](https://savoirs.rfi.fr/fr/apprendre-enseigner/langue-francaise)** — Free learning resources from Radio France Internationale, including the famous "Journal en français facile" — a daily 10-minute news bulletin adapted to B1 level. Verified free.

## Books

| Title | Author | Level | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|-------|
| Grammaire Progressive du Français | Maia Grégoire et al. | A1–C1 (multiple levels) | [Verify: confirm publisher is CLE International and current edition] — Widely recommended structured grammar reference with exercises. Multiple editions from débutant to perfectionnement. |
| Le Petit Prince | Antoine de Saint-Exupéry | B1–B2 reading | Classic literary text with accessible vocabulary; frequently used as a first authentic reading. Widely available. |
| Bescherelle — La conjugaison pour tous | Various | All levels | [Verify: confirm publisher is Hatier and current edition] — The standard French verb conjugation reference, covering all irregular verbs across all tenses. An essential desk reference. |
| 501 French Verbs | Christopher Kendris et al. | All levels | [Verify: confirm Barron's Educational Series as publisher] — Conjugation tables for 501 French verbs; useful for practice and reference. |
