# Answers: Module 01 — Quality Foundations

## Answer Key

### Easy Questions

**Q1:** QA is the practice of preventing, detecting, and communicating software quality risk across the delivery lifecycle.
**Q2:** An assertion is a precise statement that compares observed behavior with expected behavior.
**Q3:** Readable tests document intent and make failures faster to diagnose.
**Q4:** A regression is a behavior that worked before but broke after a change.
**Q5:** E2E-only strategies are often slow, brittle, and hard to debug.

### Medium Questions

**Q6:** Coverage measures what was exercised; confidence is the justified belief that important risks are controlled.
**Q7:** Risk-based selection directs limited effort toward failures with meaningful probability and impact.
**Q8:** Unit tests isolate small logic; integration tests verify collaboration across real boundaries.
**Q9:** Clear failure messages shorten diagnosis and reveal the broken product promise.
**Q10:** A bug becomes a regression test by capturing the failing scenario, expected behavior, and fix so future changes cannot silently reintroduce it.

### Hard Questions

**Q11:** Full credit requires runnable code plus an assertion tied to a clear rule.
**Q12:** Full credit explains that `assert True` tests nothing and replaces it with behavior-specific setup, action, and assertion.
**Q13:** Full credit maps isolated tax logic to unit, payment/database collaboration to integration, and user checkout to E2E.
**Q14:** Full credit mentions stable selectors, screenshots/logs/traces, deterministic data, and narrower reproduction.

### Expert Questions

**Q15:** Full credit includes unit token rules, integration email/provider or database boundaries, E2E user journey, regression checks, and tradeoff justification.
**Q16:** Full credit explains transparent communication of passing evidence, exploratory findings, impact, mitigations, and a decision recommendation.

### Bonus Questions

**Bonus 1:** Valid answers include production monitoring, support tickets, accessibility audits, performance telemetry, user analytics, or incident trends with context.
