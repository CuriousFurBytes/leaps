# Answers: Module 12 — Capstone Income Portfolio

## Answer Key

### Easy Questions
**Q1:** It means the material teaches concepts and frameworks, but does not tell a specific learner what to buy, sell, start, or file.
**Q2:** Gross revenue is money received before costs, fees, taxes, reserves, or unpaid time are considered.
**Q3:** Net income is what remains after relevant costs and reserves are subtracted.
**Q4:** Examples include platform fees, mileage, supplies, insurance, taxes, refunds, customer support, or admin time.
**Q5:** Opportunity cost is the value of the next-best use of the same time, money, or attention.

### Medium Questions
**Q6:** Gross hourly rates omit costs, unpaid time, risk, and taxes, so they can overstate the real return.
**Q7:** Active income usually requires direct labor for each dollar; semi-passive income may reduce delivery labor but still needs maintenance, marketing, compliance, and support.
**Q8:** Written rules reduce emotional decisions and prevent scaling before evidence supports the risk.
**Q9:** Records convert memory and excitement into evidence about revenue, costs, time, defects, and customer behavior.
**Q10:** Tax filings, legal structure, licenses, insurance, securities decisions, and high-risk commitments require official or professional verification.

### Hard Questions
**Q11:** $900 - $210 - $180 = $510 estimated spendable income.
**Q12:** A correct answer defines variables, subtracts costs and reserve from revenue, divides by hours, and handles nonzero hours.
**Q13:** The bug is treating gross revenue as spendable income; subtract costs and reserves before spending.
**Q14:** Good answers name risks such as platform dependency and missing tax records, then controls such as alternate channels and weekly bookkeeping.

### Expert Questions
**Q15:** A strong pilot has a small budget, defined customer, time box, evidence target, success threshold, and stop criteria.
**Q16:** A strong memo combines net economics, named risks, verification needs, and a continue/pause/stop decision.
