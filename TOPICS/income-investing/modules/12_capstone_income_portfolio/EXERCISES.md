# Exercises: Module 12 — Capstone Income Portfolio

## Instructions

Complete each exercise in order. Exercises increase in difficulty. Educational examples are not financial advice.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** State your constraints.

List time, cash-at-risk, ethical, tax/legal, and family constraints for the capstone.

---

### Exercise 2
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Inventory income ideas.

Create a table of at least five candidate income streams and classify each as active, semi-passive, or investment-related.

---

### Exercise 3
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Set advice boundaries.

Write three decisions you will not make without professional or official-source verification.

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Compare evidence quality.

Score each idea from 1–5 on evidence, fit, downside, and maintenance burden.

### Exercise 5
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Design a pilot.

Choose one side-gig idea and define a four-week pilot with budget, success criteria, and stop criteria.

### Exercise 6
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Draft an investment policy outline.

Write contribution, diversification, fee, rebalancing, and behavior rules without naming specific securities as recommendations.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Build the portfolio plan.

Combine one active experiment, one semi-passive system, and one educational investment-policy draft into a coherent plan.

### Exercise 8
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Stress-test the plan.

Describe what happens if revenue is 50% lower than expected, a platform account is closed, or markets fall sharply.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9
**Difficulty:** Expert
**Objective:** Present a decision-ready artifact.

Produce the final capstone document with assumptions, evidence, risks, controls, review cadence, and professional-verification questions.
