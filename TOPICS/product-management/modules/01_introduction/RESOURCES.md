## Essential Reading for This Module

1. **[SVPG — The Role of the Product Manager](https://www.svpg.com/the-role-of-the-product-manager/)** by Marty Cagan — A foundational short essay defining what a PM actually does. Read before or alongside Module 01.

2. **[Good Product Manager / Bad Product Manager](https://a16z.com/good-product-manager-bad-product-manager/)** by Ben Horowitz (originally written 1996, published on a16z.com) — A classic essay on the PM role that remains highly relevant. Written in direct, contrasting pairs that make the points concrete.

3. **[Shape Up, Chapter 1: Introduction](https://basecamp.com/shapeup/0.3-chapter-01)** — Basecamp's framing of the product management problem. Contrarian but grounded. Free online.
