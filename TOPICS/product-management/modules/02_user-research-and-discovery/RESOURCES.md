## Essential Reading

1. **[Teresa Torres — Continuous Discovery Habits](https://www.producttalk.org/)** — Her blog and book are the primary source for the continuous discovery and opportunity solution tree frameworks covered in this module.

2. **[The Clayton Christensen Institute — Jobs to be Done](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/jobs-to-be-done/)** — The original Jobs-to-be-Done framework explained. The milkshake study is described here.

3. **[SVPG — Product Discovery](https://www.svpg.com/product-discovery/)** by Marty Cagan — Short article on how SVPG thinks about discovery.
