## Books

| Title | Author | Level | Format | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|-------|
| *Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love* (2nd ed., 2017) | Marty Cagan | Beginner–Intermediate | Print/eBook | The canonical Silicon Valley PM reference. Covers empowered teams, discovery vs. delivery, and the PM's relationship with design and engineering. Start here. |
| *Continuous Discovery Habits* (2021) | Teresa Torres | Intermediate | Print/eBook | Systematic framework for weekly customer interviews and opportunity solution trees. Highly practical. |
| *Shape Up: Stop Running in Circles and Ship Work that Matters* | Ryan Singer (Basecamp) | Intermediate | Free online | A contrarian take on PM and delivery from the creators of Basecamp. Free at basecamp.com/shapeup. Covers appetite, shaping, and betting. |
| *Intercom on Product Management* | Intercom team | Beginner | Free eBook | Free ebook from Intercom covering PM fundamentals, prioritization, and working with customers. [Verify: search "Intercom on Product Management pdf" — has been publicly available as free download] |
| *The Lean Startup* (2011) | Eric Ries | Beginner–Intermediate | Print/eBook | Introduced MVP, build-measure-learn loop, and validated learning to mainstream product practice. Essential historical context. |
| *Escaping the Build Trap* (2018) | Melissa Perri | Intermediate | Print/eBook | Focuses on the organizational dysfunction that leads teams to measure output instead of outcomes. Strong complement to *Inspired*. |

## Free Online Resources

- **[Shape Up by Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/shapeup)** — Full text of Ryan Singer's book, free. Covers how Basecamp thinks about product work, from shaping bets to building in 6-week cycles.
- **[Silicon Valley Product Group (SVPG) Articles](https://www.svpg.com/articles/)** — Marty Cagan's ongoing writing on product management. Covers empowered teams, product discovery, and product culture.
- **[Teresa Torres's blog](https://www.producttalk.org/)** — The home of continuous discovery habits methodology. Free articles alongside the book.
- **[Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/)** — Weekly newsletter and podcast covering PM best practices, career advice, and case studies from practitioners at top companies. Some content free, archive paywalled.

## Frameworks and Reference Documents

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Jobs to be Done (JTBD) | Clayton Christensen's framework; formalized by Bob Moesta and Chris Spiek. Core idea: people "hire" products to do a job. Multiple free explanations online; Christensen's book *The Innovator's Solution* is the primary source. |
| OKR framework | Popularized at Intel by Andy Grove, then at Google. John Doerr's *Measure What Matters* (2018) is the standard reference. Many free OKR templates available online. |
| The Opportunity Solution Tree | Teresa Torres's visual framework for connecting outcomes to opportunities to solutions. Detailed in *Continuous Discovery Habits* and freely described on producttalk.org. |
| RICE Prioritization | Intercom's public blog post introducing RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) scoring — search "Intercom RICE" for the original. |
