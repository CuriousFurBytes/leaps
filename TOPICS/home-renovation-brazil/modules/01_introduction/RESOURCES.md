## Official Standards

- **ABNT NBR 15575:2013** — Edificações habitacionais — Desempenho. The performance standard covering structural, thermal, acoustic, and waterproofing requirements. Relevant to understanding what "good construction" means in Brazil. Available from [https://www.abnt.org.br](https://www.abnt.org.br)
- **NR-18 (Ministry of Labour)** — Condições e Meio Ambiente de Trabalho na Indústria da Construção. The occupational safety standard for construction sites. Freely available at [https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego](https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego) (search "NR-18"). Relevant background on PPE requirements.

## Videos and Online Tutorials

- **[Verify: search YouTube "tipos de construção residencial Brasil"]** — Multiple channels cover alvenaria convencional vs. alvenaria estrutural with visual comparisons. Look for content from technical channels associated with SENAI or university extension programs.
- **[Verify: search YouTube "como ler planta baixa"]** — Step-by-step floor plan reading tutorials in Portuguese. Multiple free videos available.
- **[Verify: search YouTube "EPI para construção civil"]** — Video tutorials on correct PPE selection and use for Brazilian construction workers.

## Reference Guides

- **Tabela de Composições de Preços para Orçamentos (TCPO) — PINI Editorial** — [Verify: search "TCPO PINI"] — The standard cost estimation reference in Brazil. Contains unit prices for labor, materials, and equipment. Essential for Module 02 (budgeting) but introduced here.
- **SINAPI (Caixa Econômica Federal)** — [https://www.caixa.gov.br/poder-publico/modernizacao-gestao/sinapi](https://www.caixa.gov.br/poder-publico/modernizacao-gestao/sinapi) — Free government price index for construction. Downloadable spreadsheets with regional unit prices.

## Tools for Practice

- **Graph paper or CAD tools for plan drawing** — For Exercise 4 and the Expert Exercise. Free online tools: [https://app.diagrams.net](https://app.diagrams.net) (draw.io) supports floor plan symbols; [https://www.roomsketcher.com](https://www.roomsketcher.com) has a free tier. Alternatively, plain graph paper (1 cm = 1 m at 1:100 scale) works for practice.

## For Further Depth

- **[Verify: "Auto-Construção no Brasil" — academic literature]** — Multiple sociology and architecture research papers analyze the *auto-construção* phenomenon in Brazil. Search Google Scholar for "auto-construção habitação brasil" for peer-reviewed Brazilian sources.
- **Conselho de Arquitetura e Urbanismo (CAU)** — [https://www.caubr.gov.br](https://www.caubr.gov.br) — RRT registry and architect lookup; useful for verifying professional credentials.
- **CREA (Confea)** — [https://www.confea.org.br](https://www.confea.org.br) — ART registry and engineer lookup; use to verify ART authenticity via the registration number.
