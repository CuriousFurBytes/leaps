## Official Standards and Regulations

### ABNT NBR Norms (Brazilian Technical Standards)

The following norms are the primary regulatory references for residential construction in Brazil.
They are available for purchase from ABNT at [https://www.abnt.org.br](https://www.abnt.org.br)
(many can also be found through university library access or via ABNT Catálogo).

| Norm | Title | Relevance |
|------|-------|-----------|
| NBR 5410:2004 | Instalações elétricas de baixa tensão | Electrical — all residential wiring |
| NBR 5626:2020 | Sistemas prediais de água fria e água quente | Water supply systems |
| NBR 8160:1999 | Sistemas prediais de esgoto sanitário | Sanitary drainage |
| NBR 6118:2014 | Projeto de estruturas de concreto | Structural concrete design |
| NBR 7190:1997 | Projeto de estruturas de madeira | Wood structures |
| NBR 15575:2013 | Edificações habitacionais — Desempenho | Residential performance (thermal, acoustic, waterproofing) |
| NBR 9050:2020 | Acessibilidade a edificações | Accessibility |
| NBR 13281:2005 | Argamassa para assentamento e revestimento | Mortar for masonry and wall finishes |
| NBR 14037:2011 | Diretrizes para elaboração de manuais de uso | Building maintenance manuals |

### Government and Regulatory Bodies

- **CREA (Conselho Regional de Engenharia e Agronomia):** [https://www.confea.org.br](https://www.confea.org.br) — Professional registration for engineers; ART issuance
- **CAU (Conselho de Arquitetura e Urbanismo):** [https://www.caubr.gov.br](https://www.caubr.gov.br) — Professional registration for architects; RRT issuance
- **ANEEL (Agência Nacional de Energia Elétrica):** [https://www.aneel.gov.br](https://www.aneel.gov.br) — Regulates solar power grid connection (Resolução Normativa 482/2012 and updates)
- **INMETRO:** [https://www.inmetro.gov.br](https://www.inmetro.gov.br) — Product certification; look for INMETRO seal on electrical materials, pipes, and waterproofing products

## Books and Manuals

> [!NOTE]
> The following books are widely used in Brazilian technical education. Confirm current
> editions and publishers before purchasing — Brazilian technical publishing changes frequently.

- **"Manual do Proprietário" (Sinduscon-SP)** — [Verify: Sinduscon-SP publishes regional building maintenance manuals; search sinduscon.org.br] — Standard maintenance manual framework used by construction companies
- **"Alvenaria de Vedação em Blocos Cerâmicos" (IPT/ABCP)** — Technical manual on ceramic block masonry from Instituto de Pesquisas Tecnológicas; covers mixing, laying, and finishes
- **"Instalações Elétricas" by Helio Creder** — [Verify: widely cited Brazilian electrical installations textbook] — Standard reference for NBR 5410 applications in residential and commercial buildings
- **"Instalações Hidráulicas e o Projeto de Arquitetura" by Renato Creder** — [Verify: companion volume to the electrical installations text] — Hydraulic systems design for residential buildings
