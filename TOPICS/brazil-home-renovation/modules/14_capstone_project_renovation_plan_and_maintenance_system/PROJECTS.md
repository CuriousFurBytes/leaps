# Projects: Module 14 — Capstone Project: Renovation Plan and Maintenance System

> Projects turn module concepts into practical renovation artifacts. Do not perform unsafe work or regulated work without qualified professionals.

## Capstone Project

Create a renovation dossier and maintenance system for a real or realistic Brazilian residence.

### Deliverables

- Existing-condition photo and measurement log.
- Scope, exclusions, and assumptions.
- Risk register and stop-work triggers.
- Budget with contingency and verification-dependent items.
- Schedule with inspection hold points.
- Standards and approvals verification log.
- Maintenance calendar and handover checklist.

### Acceptance Criteria

- No exact standard clause is asserted without verification.
- High-risk work is routed to qualified professionals.
- Climate and material choices match the stated city/state exposure.
- The final package can be reviewed by a homeowner and a licensed professional.
