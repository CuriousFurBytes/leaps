# Exercises: Module 01 — Orientation, Safety, and Planning

## Instructions

Complete each exercise in order. Exercises increase in difficulty. Use markdown, photos, sketches, or spreadsheets as appropriate, but do not perform unsafe work.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Identify visible renovation risks.

List ten visible conditions in a room that should be photographed before work begins.

---

### Exercise 2
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Practice standards-as-verification language.

Write five placeholders for Brazilian standards, municipal rules, condominium rules, utility approvals, or professional sign-off.

---

### Exercise 3
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Build a basic material-climate checklist.

Create a two-column table matching local exposures to material concerns.

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Separate facts from assumptions.

Inspect a real or fictional bathroom and write a fact/assumption/verification table.

### Exercise 5
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Draft a risk register.

Create five risk entries using this format:

```yaml
risk:
  description: ""
  consequence: ""
  mitigation: ""
  verify: ""
```

### Exercise 6
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Communicate scope clearly.

Write a one-page scope note for a small renovation, including exclusions and stop-work triggers.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Build a sequencing plan.

Create a safe sequence for renovating a wet area from documentation through handover.

### Exercise 8
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Compare material choices.

Compare two finish systems for a humid Brazilian climate and justify the safer choice.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9
**Difficulty:** Expert
**Objective:** Synthesize safety, climate, budget, and verification.

Prepare a mini renovation control plan with risk register, budget headings, schedule phases, verification log, and maintenance checklist.
