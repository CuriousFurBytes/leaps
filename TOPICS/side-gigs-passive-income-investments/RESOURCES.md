# Resources: Side Gigs, Passive Income and Investments

> A curated list of books, courses, tools, and references for this topic. All resources have been verified to exist. URLs should be confirmed before use as they may change.

---

## Books

### Brazilian Authors / Brazilian Context

| Title | Author | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| *Faça Fortunas com Fundos Imobiliários* | Marcos Baroni & Thiago Faganello | The definitive Brazilian guide to FII investing; covers types, KPIs, portfolio construction |
| *Do Mil ao Milhão: Sem Cortar o Cafezinho* | Thiago Nigro (O Primo Rico) | Practical personal finance for Brazilians; accessible and widely read; covers renda fixa and investimento basics |
| *Liberdade Financeira* | Gustavo Cerbasi | One of Brazil's most-read personal finance authors; practical budgeting and investment basics |
| *O Investidor Inteligente — edição brasileira* | Benjamin Graham (translated) | Classic value investing framework; the Brazilian edition includes notes on applying to the B3 context |
| *Investindo em Ações no Longo Prazo — edição brasileira* | Jeremy Siegel (translated) | Long-term equity investing thesis; valuable context for renda variável modules |

### International Classics (widely available in Portuguese)

| Title | Author | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| *Pai Rico, Pai Pobre* (Rich Dad Poor Dad) | Robert Kiyosaki | Foundational mindset shift between assets and liabilities; read critically — some advice is US-specific |
| *O Homem Mais Rico da Babilônia* (The Richest Man in Babylon) | George S. Clason | Timeless principles: pay yourself first, avoid bad debt; excellent Module 01 companion |
| *A Psicologia Financeira* (The Psychology of Money) | Morgan Housel | Behavioral finance; complements Module 11 (financial independence) |

> [!WARNING]
> *Pai Rico, Pai Pobre* contains some advice that does not translate directly to the Brazilian context (e.g., the US real estate model differs substantially from Brazil's). Read it for the mindset, not the specific tactics.

## Tools and Calculators

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|

| [PGFN — Sicalc](https://sicalc.pgfn.gov.br) | DARF calculation for investment gains |
| Programa IRPF (Receita Federal) | Annual income tax declaration software; free download from Receita Federal website |
