### Starter Project: Apply This Module's Framework

**Time:** 2–4 hours  
**Deliverable:** Written analysis or spreadsheet

Apply the core framework from this module to your personal situation:

1. Review the module's Key Concepts and identify which 3 are most relevant to your current financial situation
2. Find one real-world example of each key concept in Brazilian financial media (Infomoney, Valor Econômico, or similar)
3. Calculate one worked example from the module's domain using your own numbers
4. Write a one-paragraph action plan: what one thing will you do differently as a result of this module?

**Success criteria:** Your analysis is specific to your real situation, uses real numbers, and produces at least one actionable decision.
