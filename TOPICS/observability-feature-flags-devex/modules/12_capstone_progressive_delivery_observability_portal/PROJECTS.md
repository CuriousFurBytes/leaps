# Projects: Module 12 — Capstone Project: Progressive Delivery Observability Portal

## Capstone Project Brief

Build a Progressive Delivery Observability Portal that helps a team understand active flags, rollout stage, owners, guardrail status, linked dashboards, error streams, product analytics, and rollback instructions.

## Milestones

1. Define the data model for flags, owners, environments, guardrails, and dashboard links.
2. Build or document a view that shows rollout health at a glance.
3. Add a decision log for advance, pause, rollback, and cleanup decisions.
4. Add a getting-started guide for a new engineer using the portal.
5. Present tradeoffs: vendor lock-in, privacy, cost, alert fatigue, and operational ownership.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The artifact is usable by a realistic engineering team.
- It synthesizes feature flags, PostHog, Datadog, Sentry, Grafana, monitoring, and DevEx.
- It includes staged help but does not provide a copy-paste final solution.
