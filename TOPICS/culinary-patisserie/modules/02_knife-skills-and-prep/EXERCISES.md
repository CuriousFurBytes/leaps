# Exercises: Module 02 — Knife Skills and Prep

> **STUB** — Full exercises to be written when this module is fully developed.

## Instructions

Complete each exercise in order. For knife skills, repetition is essential — aim to perform
each cut exercise at least three times before moving to the next module.

---

## Preview of Exercise Topics

1. **Easy** — Identify knife cuts from photographs; match cut name to dimensions
2. **Easy** — Practice the pinch grip and claw grip (no cutting — just grip awareness)
3. **Easy** — Chiffonade a bunch of basil
4. **Medium** — Produce a 1 cm medium dice of one onion, one carrot, and one stalk of celery
5. **Medium** — Produce a julienne and brunoise of one medium carrot
6. **Medium** — Prepare a classic mirepoix for a stock (from Module 03)
7. **Hard** — Produce a fine brunoise of 200 g of any root vegetable; all pieces must be
   uniform; photograph result
8. **Hard** — Execute a full prep mise en place for a three-component recipe
9. **Expert** — Tourné eight medium potatoes; consistency is the mark of skill
