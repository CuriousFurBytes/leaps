# Exercises: Module 01 — Orientation and Mise en Place

## Instructions

Complete each exercise in order. Exercises increase in difficulty. Submit answers by editing this file or adding a separate solutions file.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Identify core vocabulary.

Define five key terms from this module in your own words.

### Exercise 2
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Practice mise en place planning.

Write a tool and ingredient checklist for one preparation covered by this module.

### Exercise 3
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Record sensory observations.

Taste a simple pastry component and describe appearance, aroma, texture, flavor, and finish.

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Convert a preparation into a repeatable record.

Write a gram-based formula and method card for a small component.

### Exercise 5
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Connect method to outcome.

Explain how one step in the method changes the final texture.

### Exercise 6
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Improve a workflow.

Create a short production timeline that includes setup, active work, rest, cooling, and cleanup.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Troubleshoot from evidence.

Given a failed product, list three likely causes and one controlled retest for each.

### Exercise 8
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Apply professional judgment.

Revise a preparation for a different service context, such as takeaway, plated service, or buffet holding.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9
**Difficulty:** Expert
**Objective:** Synthesize technique, production, and evaluation.

Create a complete mini production plan for one finished pastry item, including formula, method, timing, quality checks, and a one-paragraph troubleshooting plan.
