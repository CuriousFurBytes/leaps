## Books

1. **"On Food and Cooking" by Harold McGee** (revised edition 2004) — Chapter 14 covers the
   science of heat in cooking in remarkable depth. McGee's explanations of the Maillard reaction,
   conduction, and convection are the scientific foundation for everything in this module.
   [Verify: confirm edition and chapter number before sharing]

2. **"The Food Lab" by J. Kenji López-Alt** (2015, W.W. Norton) — The introduction and early
   chapters explain López-Alt's approach to scientific testing in the home kitchen. His sections
   on searing, the Maillard reaction, and seasoning directly complement this module.
   [Verify: confirm page numbers for specific sections]

3. **"Jacques Pépin: New Complete Techniques"** — Pépin's treatment of mise en place and basic
   kitchen organisation represents the classical French approach at its clearest.
   [Verify: confirm current edition and publisher]
