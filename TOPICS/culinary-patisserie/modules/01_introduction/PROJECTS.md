# Projects: Module 01 — Introduction to the Kitchen

> Projects for this module focus on applying mise en place, heat science, and flavour
> principles in real kitchen work. Complete at least one project to earn 15 points.

---

## Project 1-A: The Mise en Place Challenge (Beginner)

**Objective:** Build the mise en place habit by cooking three different meals over one week
using a complete written mise en place checklist for each.

**Deliverable:**
For each of three meals, prepare and submit:
1. A written mise en place checklist (prepared before cooking begins)
2. A photograph of your station before you begin cooking
3. A brief reflection (3–5 sentences) on how the mise en place affected the cooking experience

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Checklist includes equipment AND measured ingredients
- Station photograph shows organised, logical layout
- Reflection demonstrates genuine engagement with the discipline

---

## Project 1-B: The Heat Method Showcase (Intermediate)

**Objective:** Cook one ingredient (choose from: potato, carrot, chicken, or fish) using all
three heat-transfer methods and compare the results.

**Deliverable:**
1. Cook the chosen ingredient three times using different methods (e.g., pan-seared/conduction,
   boiled/convection, grilled under a broiler/radiation)
2. Photograph all three results side by side
3. Write a 200-word comparison: texture, colour, flavour, aroma, and time/effort

**Evaluation criteria:**
- Clear identification of which heat mechanism applies to each method
- Honest sensory comparison (not just "this one was better")
- Understanding of why the results differ, grounded in the module theory

## Project 1-C: The Flavour Balance Menu (Expert)

**Objective:** Design and cook a three-course meal where each course deliberately targets a
specific flavour contrast or balance challenge.

**Specification:**
- Course 1 (starter): predominantly umami + acid
- Course 2 (main): a dish where the final balance required three adjustments during cooking
  (document each adjustment and why you made it)
- Course 3 (dessert): sweet + bitter + salt (the classic bitter chocolate / fleur de sel
  pairing as a foundation)

**Deliverable:**
1. Written menu card with dish names and brief descriptions
2. Cooking log for each course: what adjustments did you make and why?
3. Three photographs (one per course, plated)
4. Reflection: which taste was hardest to balance and why?
