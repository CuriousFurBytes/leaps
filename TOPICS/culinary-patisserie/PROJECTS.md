## Beginner Projects

### Project B-1: The Compound Butter Collection
**Modules:** 01–02
**Deliverable:** Prepare three different compound butters (e.g., herb butter, blue cheese
butter, lemon-caper butter). Roll each in clingfilm, label, and photograph. Write tasting notes
for each.

### Project B-2: Vinaigrette Variations
**Modules:** 01
**Deliverable:** Prepare four vinaigrettes using the 3:1 oil-to-acid ratio as a base,
varying the acid (red wine vinegar, lemon, balsamic, sherry vinegar) and adding one
emulsifier (mustard) to two of them. Compare the texture and stability of emulsified vs.
broken vinaigrettes. Document results.

### Project B-3: Knife Skills Showcase
**Modules:** 02
**Deliverable:** Photograph ten plates demonstrating each of the seven standard cuts
using three different vegetables. Caption each photo with cut name, vegetable, and use case.

## Intermediate Projects

### Project I-1: The Mother Sauce Series
**Modules:** 03
**Deliverable:** Over one week, prepare all five mother sauces from scratch on separate
days. For each, produce one derivative sauce. Document ratios, technique notes, and one
photograph per sauce.

### Project I-2: A Perfect Roast Chicken
**Modules:** 04
**Deliverable:** Roast a whole chicken using dry-brine and high-heat technique. Rest
correctly. Carve and present. Write a technical account of the cooking process — temperatures,
times, visual cues, and flavour assessment.

### Project I-3: Egg Techniques Tasting Plate
**Modules:** 05
**Deliverable:** Prepare and photograph five egg preparations on one plate:
scrambled (French style), poached, fried (sunny side up), soft-boiled, and a rolled omelette.
Write a technical note explaining the temperature and protein coagulation principles for each.

## Advanced Projects

### Project A-1: Sourdough Loaf — Week-Long Project
**Modules:** 06
**Deliverable:** Maintain a sourdough starter for seven days, bake one loaf, and document
the process with daily photographs of the starter activity and a final crumb shot. Include
the baker's percentage formula you used and reflect on the result.

### Project A-2: Tarte aux Pommes Classique
**Modules:** 07
**Deliverable:** Produce a classic apple tart from scratch: make pâte sucrée, blind-bake
the shell, prepare a pastry cream filling, arrange and glaze apple slices. Photograph and
write a technical review of your dough technique and any adjustments you would make next time.

### Project A-3: Ganache Truffle Box
**Modules:** 08
**Deliverable:** Temper dark chocolate, produce a ganache with at least two flavour
variations (e.g., vanilla-sea salt, raspberry), mould or hand-roll twelve truffles, and
coat in tempered chocolate or cocoa powder. Photograph the finished box and record the
tempered chocolate's snap test result.

## Expert Projects

### Project E-1: A Three-Course Dinner Party
**Modules:** 03–05, 09, 11
**Deliverable:** Plan, cost, and execute a three-course dinner for at least four people.
Menu must include a sauce derived from a mother sauce, one pastry or custard element, and
a plated dessert. Submit: menu card, food cost calculation, and one photograph per course.

### Project E-2: Menu Engineering Analysis
**Modules:** 11
**Deliverable:** Take a real restaurant menu (use a publicly available menu). Classify each
dish as Star, Plough Horse, Puzzle, or Dog using the Boston Consulting Group matrix approach
applied to food cost and popularity. Write a 500-word analysis with recommendations.

## Capstone Project

### Project C-1: The Tasting Menu
**Modules:** All (01–12)
**Deliverable:** See [Module 12: Capstone Project](./modules/12_capstone-project/README.md)
for the full brief.

Design, cook, plate, photograph, and document a complete 4-course tasting menu:
- **Amuse-bouche** — one bite, conceptually provocative
- **Entrée** — first course, light; demonstrates sauce and prep skills
- **Plat principal** — main course; demonstrates cooking method mastery and plating
- **Dessert** — applies at least one patisserie technique from Modules 07–09

This is the definitive project for this topic. It synthesises every module.
