# Exercises: Module 02 — Customer Problems and Outcomes

## Instructions

Complete each exercise in order. Exercises increase in difficulty. Submit your answers by editing this file or committing a solutions file.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Identify core vocabulary.

Write one sentence each for customer, problem, outcome, assumption, and risk.

### Exercise 2

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Reframe output as outcome.

Convert this feature request into a problem statement: "Add a bulk-edit button."

```yaml
request: "Add a bulk-edit button"
customer: ""
problem: ""
outcome: ""
```

### Exercise 3

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Objective:** Spot discovery methods.

List one method for learning about value risk, usability risk, feasibility risk, and viability risk.

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Structure an opportunity brief.

Create a one-page opportunity brief for a product you use weekly.

### Exercise 5

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Connect evidence to decisions.

Given three pieces of evidence, state whether each supports, weakens, or does not affect the opportunity.

### Exercise 6

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Objective:** Practice stakeholder communication.

Write a six-bullet update that explains what the team learned, what changed, and what decision is needed.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7

**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Diagnose a flawed discovery plan.

Find three problems in this plan and rewrite it.

```markdown
We will ask users if they like the idea, show leadership the positive quotes, and begin engineering next sprint.
```

### Exercise 8

**Difficulty:** Hard
**Objective:** Build a risk-ranked learning plan.

Rank five assumptions by risk and choose the next two discovery activities.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9

**Difficulty:** Expert
**Objective:** Synthesize product, design, and discovery judgment.

Write a decision memo recommending build, test more, pivot, or stop for a realistic opportunity. Include confidence level, evidence, risks, and next step.
