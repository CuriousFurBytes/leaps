# Projects: Module 02 — Customer Problems and Outcomes

## Project 1: Opportunity Brief

Create a one-page opportunity brief for a real product or service problem. Include customer, problem, outcome, assumptions, evidence needed, and decision owner.

## Project 2: Discovery Plan

Design a two-week discovery plan that uses at least two methods and explains which risk each method addresses.

## Project 3: Stakeholder Decision Memo

Write a memo that recommends build, test more, pivot, or stop. Include what changed because of evidence.

## Capstone Connection

Save your best artifacts. The final capstone asks you to combine these skills into a portfolio-quality discovery case.
