# Answers: Module 03 — Design Research and Interviewing

## Answer Key

### Easy Questions

**Q1:** Product discovery is structured learning that reduces uncertainty about what to build, for whom, and why.
**Q2:** A product outcome is a measurable behavior or condition that changes because the product work succeeds.
**Q3:** Examples include framing opportunities, aligning stakeholders, prioritizing risks, defining outcomes, and partnering with design and engineering.
**Q4:** Value risk asks whether customers care; feasibility risk asks whether the team can build and operate it.
**Q5:** They make assumptions visible so users, designers, engineers, and stakeholders can critique them before expensive delivery.

### Medium Questions

**Q6:** A feature request names a possible solution; a customer problem names the struggle or unmet need that might justify many possible solutions.
**Q7:** Approval means people accepted a proposal; alignment means they share context about goals, evidence, tradeoffs, risks, and next decisions.
**Q8:** Interviews, prototypes, analytics, engineering spikes, and legal reviews answer different kinds of uncertainty.
**Q9:** Usability research emphasizes observing human behavior; agile delivery emphasizes iteration and feedback. Discovery combines both before and during delivery.
**Q10:** Any clear assumption, such as "administrators check this workflow daily," earns credit if testable before building.

### Hard Questions

**Q11:** Full credit reframes the request into a specific customer, problem, outcome, and evidence-gathering step.
**Q12:** Full credit notes stated preference bias, weak threshold logic, missing segment definition, and lack of behavioral evidence.
**Q13:** Full credit includes customer, problem, outcome, assumptions, risks, and next step in valid YAML-style structure.
**Q14:** Full credit chooses expert review, policy review, threat/privacy analysis, or compliance consultation and explains why customer opinion is insufficient.

### Expert Questions

**Q15:** Full credit integrates risk ranking, research, prototype testing, feasibility analysis, decision criteria, and stakeholder communication.
**Q16:** Full credit makes a concise business case: delay cost, risk reduced, evidence to gather, decision date, and fallback plan.

### Bonus Questions

**Bonus 1:** Full credit identifies an ethical risk such as consent, privacy, manipulation, accessibility exclusion, or biased sampling and gives a mitigation.
