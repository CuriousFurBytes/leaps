# Projects: Product Management, Design, Discovery

## Project 1: Feature Request Reframing Pack

Collect ten feature requests from public product forums or your own experience. Rewrite each as customer, problem, outcome, assumption, and next learning step.

## Project 2: Ethical Interview Study

Plan and conduct three low-risk interviews about a common workflow. Summarize observations, themes, confidence, and open questions without overclaiming.

## Project 3: Product Decision Memo

Choose a product opportunity and write a decision memo recommending build, test more, pivot, or stop.

## Capstone Preparation

Save the strongest artifacts from each project. Module 12 combines them into a portfolio-quality discovery case.
