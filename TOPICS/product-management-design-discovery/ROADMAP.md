# Roadmap: Product Management, Design, Discovery

## Learning Path

1. Build vocabulary and role clarity in Module 01.
2. Practice customer, problem, and outcome framing in Module 02.
3. Learn ethical research and interviewing in Module 03.
4. Continue through prioritization, prototyping, analytics, strategy, delivery partnership, platform work, ethics, and operating models.
5. Finish with the Capstone Product Discovery Portfolio.

## Recommended Pace

| Pace | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Casual | One module every two weeks with one practice artifact per module. |
| Focused | One module per week with exercises and a decision memo. |
| Intensive | Two modules per week plus peer review of artifacts. |

## Portfolio Artifacts

- Opportunity brief
- Interview guide
- Research synthesis
- Prototype test plan
- Metrics plan
- Product strategy memo
- Capstone discovery portfolio
