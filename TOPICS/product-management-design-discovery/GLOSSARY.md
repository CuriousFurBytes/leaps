# Glossary: Product Management, Design, Discovery

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Structured learning that reduces uncertainty before or during delivery. |
| Outcome | A measurable change in customer, user, or business behavior. |
| Opportunity | A specific customer problem, need, or desire worth investigating. |
| Prototype | A low- or high-fidelity representation used to learn before building fully. |
| Stakeholder | Anyone with influence over, responsibility for, or exposure to a product decision. |
| Viability Risk | The risk that a solution does not work for the business, legal, support, or go-to-market model. |
| Value Risk | The risk that customers do not care enough for the solution to matter. |
