# Resources: Product Management, Design, Discovery

## Core Resources

- [Nielsen Norman Group: Design Thinking 101](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/design-thinking/) — grounding in human-centered design vocabulary.
- [Nielsen Norman Group: User Interviews](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/user-interviews/) — practical interviewing guidance.
- [Product Talk: Product Discovery Habits](https://www.producttalk.org/product-discovery-habits/) — public writing on continuous discovery practice.
- [Atlassian Team Playbook](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook) — facilitation plays for team alignment.

## Suggested Practice

- Rewrite feature requests as problem statements.
- Interview people about real past workflows without pitching a solution.
- Create one decision memo per module.
- Keep a portfolio folder of artifacts for the capstone.
