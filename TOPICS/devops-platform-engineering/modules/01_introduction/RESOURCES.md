# Resources: Module 01 — Introduction to DevOps and Platform Engineering

> Verified resources specifically relevant to this module's culture and foundational concepts.

---

## Books

| Title | Author(s) | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| The Phoenix Project | Gene Kim, Kevin Behr, George Spafford | The DevOps origin story in novel form; read this before all other DevOps books |
| Accelerate | Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim | The DORA research book; empirical evidence for which practices drive performance |
| The DevOps Handbook | Gene Kim, Jez Humble, Patrick Debois, John Willis | Practitioner implementation guide for the Three Ways |
| Team Topologies | Matthew Skelton, Manuel Pais | Organizational design for software teams; essential for Platform Engineering |
