## Module 01 Project: Your Vehicle's Profile Document

**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Estimated Time:** 1–2 hours
**When to do it:** After completing the module README and before or alongside the exercises

### Description

Create a one-page "Vehicle Profile" document for your car (or a family member's/friend's car if you don't have one). This document becomes your reference guide for all future modules.

### What to Include

1. **Basic Information**
   - Year, Make, Model, Trim level
   - VIN (found on dashboard near windshield on driver's side, or inside driver's door jamb)
   - Mileage
   - Engine type (4-cylinder? 6-cylinder? Turbocharged? Diesel?)
   - Transmission type (automatic? manual? CVT?)

2. **Fluid Specifications** (from owner's manual)
   - Engine oil: viscosity and type (e.g., "0W-20 Full Synthetic")
   - Coolant: type (green/IAT, orange/OAT, or OEM-specific?)
   - Transmission fluid: type
   - Tire pressure: front PSI, rear PSI (from door jamb sticker)

3. **Current Fluid Status** (check and record)
   - Engine oil: level and appearance
   - Coolant: level and color
   - Brake fluid: level and color
   - Tires: actual measured pressure at each corner

4. **Maintenance History** (as much as you know)
   - Last oil change: date and mileage
   - Last tire rotation: date and mileage
   - Any known issues or recent repairs

5. **Open Recalls** (check NHTSA database)
   - Any open safety recalls? (yes/no; if yes, what are they?)

### Success Criteria

- [ ] All basic vehicle information recorded
- [ ] All fluid specs confirmed from owner's manual (not guessed)
- [ ] Current fluid status physically checked and documented
- [ ] NHTSA recall check completed
- [ ] Document saved where you'll be able to reference it in future modules

### Why This Matters

This document is the foundation for every subsequent hands-on exercise. Knowing your car's oil viscosity, tire pressure spec, and coolant type means you buy the right product every time without guessing. Finding an open recall now may save you from a safety incident later.
