# Exercises: Module 01 — Safety, Tools, and Vehicle Inspection

## Instructions

Complete each exercise in order. Use your own vehicle only when it is safe, parked legally, on level ground, and you can follow the owner manual. If you do not have a vehicle available, use a sample owner manual or a trusted vehicle listing and clearly label assumptions.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Objective:** Identify vehicle-specific information.

Record year, make, model, engine or trim if known, tire size, recommended cold tire pressure, and oil specification from the owner manual or vehicle placard.

### Exercise 2

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Objective:** Build a safety checklist.

Write a ten-item checklist for preparing a safe maintenance workspace.

### Exercise 3

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Objective:** Practice maintenance documentation.

Create a maintenance log entry for a fictional inspection using this format:

```text
Date:
Mileage:
Observation:
Action:
Follow-up:
```

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Objective:** Distinguish observation from diagnosis.

Write three vehicle symptoms and list evidence you would collect before choosing a repair.

### Exercise 5

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Objective:** Apply stop/go safety judgment.

For each scenario, decide whether to continue, drive carefully to a shop, or stop driving: low tire pressure warning, brake grinding, small washer fluid leak, overheating gauge, and cracked windshield wiper blade.

### Exercise 6

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Objective:** Locate reliable information.

Compare an owner manual, a forum post, and a service manual for one maintenance question. Explain which source should control the final decision.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7

**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Objective:** Build an inspection workflow.

Design a 20-minute pre-road-trip inspection that covers tires, fluids, lights, leaks, documents, emergency supplies, and warning thresholds.

### Exercise 8

**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Objective:** Communicate with a repair shop.

Write a concise service request for a vehicle with vibration at highway speed. Include symptoms, conditions, recent work, and what you do not want assumed without diagnosis.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9

**Difficulty:** Expert  
**Objective:** Create a risk-ranked maintenance plan.

Rank ten possible maintenance issues by safety risk, likelihood, cost of delay, and whether an owner should handle them. Justify each ranking.
