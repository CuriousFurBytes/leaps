# Answers: Module 01 — Safety, Tools, and Vehicle Inspection

## Answer Key

### Easy Questions

**Q1:** The owner manual or vehicle-specific maintenance information.  
**Q2:** Safety glasses, gloves, closed-toe shoes, hearing protection, or similar appropriate PPE.  
**Q3:** Date, mileage, observation or condition, action taken, and follow-up.  
**Q4:** A jack is for lifting and can fail or tip; jack stands support the load.  
**Q5:** Brakes, steering, suspension, tires, airbags, fuel system, or high-voltage system.

### Medium Questions

**Q6:** Observation records what is noticed; diagnosis explains the verified cause.  
**Q7:** Videos can visualize tasks but may not match the exact vehicle, specification, or safety requirement.  
**Q8:** Correct torque keeps fasteners secure without under-tightening or damaging parts.  
**Q9:** Scheduled maintenance follows time or mileage; condition inspection responds to measured wear, symptoms, or environment.  
**Q10:** Stopping prevents injury, worsening damage, and unsafe driving when risk exceeds skill or tools.

### Hard Questions

**Q11:** Should include tires, pressure, tread, lights, fluid levels, leaks, wipers, warning lights, documents, emergency kit, and stop thresholds.  
**Q12:** Should include tire pressure, road condition, brake noise, pedal feel, fluid level, recent work, tire wear, steering vibration, and warning lights.  
**Q13:** Must replace jack-only support with jack stands, use approved lift points, chock wheels, torque lug nuts to spec, and avoid unsafe test driving.  
**Q14:** Strong answers include date, mileage, measured pressures, tire position, inflation action, recurrence, and shop follow-up for leak inspection.

### Expert Questions

**Q15:** Strong answers classify tasks by safety criticality, required tools, legal constraints, symptoms, and ability to verify the result.  
**Q16:** Strong answers explain that records preserve evidence, safety ranks urgency, and information hierarchy prevents generic guesses from becoming incorrect repairs.

### Bonus Questions

**Bonus 1:** Strong answers discuss intervals, records, inspections, trend tracking, risk ranking, and planned downtime.
