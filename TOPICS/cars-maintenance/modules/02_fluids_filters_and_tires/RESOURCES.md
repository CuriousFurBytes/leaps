# Resources: Module 02 — Fluids, Filters, and Tires

> Resources listed here are specific to this module's content. Verify vehicle-specific details against the owner manual or factory service information.

## Primary Resource

- Your vehicle owner manual — best first reference for routine maintenance, warnings, tire placards, fluids, and service intervals.

## Official and Safety References

- [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](https://www.nhtsa.gov/) — recalls, safety issues, and vehicle safety information.
- [TireWise from NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/equipment/tires) — tire safety basics and consumer guidance.
- [OSHA Motor Vehicle Safety](https://www.osha.gov/motor-vehicle-safety) — workplace-oriented safety concepts that reinforce hazard awareness.

## Practice Ideas

- Read the maintenance schedule for one vehicle and summarize the next three upcoming services.
- Find the tire placard and compare it to the tire sidewall maximum pressure.
- Photograph or sketch safe jacking points from the owner manual without lifting the vehicle.
