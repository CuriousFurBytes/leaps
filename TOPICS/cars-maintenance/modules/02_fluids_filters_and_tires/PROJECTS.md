# Projects: Module 02 — Fluids, Filters, and Tires

## Mini Project: Owner Maintenance Binder

Create a concise maintenance binder for one vehicle or fictional vehicle.

### Requirements

- Vehicle identification summary.
- Safety checklist.
- Maintenance log template.
- Tire and fluid reference page.
- Stop-and-seek-help decision page.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Uses vehicle-specific information where available.
- Separates facts from assumptions.
- Includes at least five safety warnings relevant to the vehicle.
- Avoids giving a complete repair procedure for high-risk work.
