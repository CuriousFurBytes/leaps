# Resources: Module 12 — Capstone Project

> Use these resources to support the capstone. Verify details against the exact vehicle.

## Primary Resource

- Your vehicle owner manual — maintenance intervals, warning lights, tire placards, fluids, and normal owner procedures.

## Official and Safety References

- [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](https://www.nhtsa.gov/) — recalls and safety research.
- [NHTSA Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) — vehicle-specific recall lookup.
- [TireWise from NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/equipment/tires) — tire safety guidance.

## Project Support

- Use a calendar or plain Markdown table for the 12-month plan.
- Use a simple risk scale from 1 to 5 for safety severity, likelihood, and cost of delay.
- Keep shop communication templates factual and symptom-based.
