# Answers: Module 12 — Capstone Project

## Answer Key

### Easy Questions

**Q1:** A complete vehicle maintenance program.  
**Q2:** Examples include brake warning, overheating, oil pressure warning, severe tire damage, fuel smell, or unstable steering.  
**Q3:** The next 12 months or 12,000 miles.  
**Q4:** Assumptions prevent guessed information from being mistaken for vehicle-specific fact.  
**Q5:** Vehicle profile, safety rules, inspection checklist, service schedule, maintenance log, shop template, risk register, or budget priorities.

### Medium Questions

**Q6:** Concise checklists are more likely to be used correctly under real conditions.  
**Q7:** Owner-serviceable tasks are low-risk and verifiable; professional tasks require specialized tools, training, legal authority, or safety controls.  
**Q8:** Some issues become much more expensive or dangerous if delayed, so urgency is not only current severity.  
**Q9:** It records symptoms, conditions, recent work, and diagnostic boundaries instead of inviting guesses.  
**Q10:** Dangerous systems require trained procedures; owners should record evidence and set stop thresholds.

### Hard Questions

**Q11:** Strong answers rank each issue and justify safety, likelihood, delay cost, and owner suitability.  
**Q12:** Strong answers include conditions, frequency, sounds, lights, battery age if known, recent work, and requested diagnosis.  
**Q13:** Correct answer says grinding brakes are a stop-driving or immediate-shop condition, not a gentle-driving plan.  
**Q14:** Strong answers include tires, lights, fluids, leaks, wipers, warnings, documents, battery, belts or hoses, and emergency gear.

### Expert Questions

**Q15:** Strong answers prioritize safety-critical and do-not-drive issues first, then cost-of-delay items, then comfort or convenience work.  
**Q16:** Strong answers show how each section of the capstone supports safe, evidence-based action.

### Bonus Questions

**Bonus 1:** Strong answers add per-vehicle records, shared scheduling, tire rotation plans, budget reserves, and downtime planning.
