# Exercises: Module 12 — Capstone Project

## Instructions

Treat these exercises as project checkpoints. Do not perform physical vehicle work unless it is safe, legal, and within your training.

---

## Easy Exercises (1–3)

### Exercise 1

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Objective:** Define the capstone vehicle.

Create the vehicle profile with information sources and assumptions.

### Exercise 2

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Objective:** Establish safety boundaries.

Write ten stop-work or stop-driving triggers.

### Exercise 3

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Objective:** Prepare a maintenance log.

Create a reusable log template.

## Medium Exercises (4–6)

### Exercise 4

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Objective:** Build recurring checklists.

Draft monthly, seasonal, and pre-trip checklists.

### Exercise 5

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Objective:** Plan scheduled service.

Convert owner-manual intervals into a 12-month service calendar.

### Exercise 6

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Objective:** Communicate symptoms clearly.

Write two shop request examples: one for vibration and one for intermittent starting.

## Hard Exercises (7–8)

### Exercise 7

**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Objective:** Rank risk.

Build a 15-item risk register with safety, likelihood, cost of delay, and owner suitability.

### Exercise 8

**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Objective:** Audit unsafe assumptions.

Review your program and identify at least five places where generic advice could be dangerous.

## Expert Exercise (9)

### Exercise 9

**Difficulty:** Expert  
**Objective:** Deliver the complete maintenance program.

Assemble the final capstone artifact and write a one-page defense of your priorities.
