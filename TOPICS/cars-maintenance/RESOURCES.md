## Official Documentation

- **[Your Vehicle's Owner's Manual]** — The single most important document for your specific car. Found in the glovebox or downloadable from the manufacturer's website by entering your VIN. Contains the factory-specified maintenance intervals, fluid specs, and torque values for your exact model and year.
- **[NHTSA Safety Ratings and Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov)** — The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration database for safety ratings, TSBs (Technical Service Bulletins), and recall information. Enter your VIN to check for open recalls on your vehicle.
- **[VehicleHistory.com / Carfax-equivalent reports]** — Paid services that reveal accident history, ownership count, mileage records, and title issues. Worth the cost before any used car purchase.

## Tools & Resources for Diagnosis

| Tool | Purpose | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| OBD-II Bluetooth Scanner | Read and clear fault codes | Budget options ($20–50) work well for basic code reading; verify OBD2 compatibility for your vehicle year (post-1996 US) |
| Tire pressure gauge | Check and set tire pressure | Dial gauges are more accurate than digital for most consumers |
| Multimeter | Test battery voltage, test circuits | Essential for any electrical diagnosis work |
| Floor jack + jack stands | Safely lift vehicle | Never work under a car supported only by a scissor jack — always use jack stands |
