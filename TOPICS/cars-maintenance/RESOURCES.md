# Resources: Cars, Maintenance, and Ownership

## Primary Resources

- Your vehicle owner manual — required first reference for routine maintenance.
- Factory service information for your exact vehicle — required for repair procedures and torque specifications.

## Safety and Consumer References

- [National Highway Traffic Safety Administration](https://www.nhtsa.gov/) — recalls and vehicle safety information.
- [NHTSA Recalls](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) — recall lookup by VIN or vehicle.
- [TireWise from NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/equipment/tires) — tire safety and consumer basics.

## Practice Resources

- A tire pressure gauge and tread depth gauge.
- A paper or Markdown maintenance log.
- A flashlight, gloves, eye protection, and the vehicle's owner manual.
