# Projects: Cars, Maintenance, and Ownership

## Topic Projects

1. **Maintenance Binder:** Build a basic owner binder with vehicle profile, safety checklist, service records, and inspection routines.
2. **Seasonal Inspection Plan:** Create a winter or summer readiness plan for tires, fluids, wipers, battery, lights, and emergency supplies.
3. **Capstone Maintenance Program:** Complete Module 12 by designing a 12-month or 12,000-mile program for one vehicle.
