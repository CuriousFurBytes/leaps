# Roadmap: Cars, Maintenance, and Ownership

## Learning Path

1. Build safety habits and learn basic inspection.
2. Practice routine owner maintenance: tires, fluids, filters, lights, and records.
3. Learn first-step diagnosis for brakes, batteries, dashboard warnings, and symptoms.
4. Progress into engines, cooling, suspension, drivetrain, electrical systems, ownership economics, and modern vehicle constraints.
5. Complete the capstone by building a full maintenance program for one vehicle.

## Completion Standard

A learner is ready to complete this topic when they can maintain records, perform low-risk checks, explain safety limits, communicate with shops, and prioritize work using safety, evidence, cost of delay, and vehicle-specific information.
