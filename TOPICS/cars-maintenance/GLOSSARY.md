# Glossary: Cars, Maintenance, and Ownership

## Terms

- **Owner manual:** Vehicle-specific guide for routine operation, maintenance, warning lights, fluids, and tire information.
- **Factory service information:** Detailed professional procedures, diagrams, specifications, and diagnostic steps for a specific vehicle.
- **Torque specification:** Required tightening value for a fastener.
- **Wear item:** Component expected to degrade through normal use, such as tires, filters, fluids, belts, wipers, and brake pads.
- **Stop-driving condition:** A symptom or warning that makes continued driving unsafe until inspected.
- **Parts-cannon diagnosis:** Replacing likely parts without verifying the actual cause.
